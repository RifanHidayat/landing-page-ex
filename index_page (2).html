Home/Activities

Things to do around your stay

From dolphins at sunrise in Lovina to sunsets in Seminyak, here are a few ideas. Our team can help you arrange them.

Lovina · Early morning

Dolphin watching at sunrise

Small boats leave Lovina Beach at dawn to look for wild dolphins, a classic North Bali experience.
Lovina · Half day

Snorkeling off Lovina

Calm, shallow reefs close to shore, good for beginners and families.
Lovina · Half day

Gitgit Waterfall

A short drive from Lovina, with a forest walk down to a tall, cool waterfall.
Lovina · 1–2 hours

Banjar Hot Springs

Soak in warm, mineral-rich pools set in a green tropical garden.
Lovina · 1–2 hours

Brahmavihara-Arama Monastery

A peaceful Buddhist monastery on a hillside with garden terraces and sea views.
Seminyak · Late afternoon

Seminyak Beach sunset

Walk the sand, then watch the sun go down from a beachfront café.
Seminyak · 2 hours

Surf lesson

Beginner-friendly waves nearby, with boards and instructors easy to arrange.
Seminyak · 1–2 hours

Spa and Balinese massage

Unwind with a traditional massage, one of the best ways to end a travel day.
Seminyak · Half day

Tanah Lot Temple at sunset

A famous sea temple on a rock, best visited in the late afternoon.
Seminyak · Full day

Ubud day trip

Rice terraces, art markets and the Monkey Forest, all in one day out.
